# EduPocket Original Audio Notes

The MP3 files in this folder are original short educational audio notes created for EduPocket.

They are not copied from third-party copyrighted material. The narration scripts were written for this project and generated locally from those original scripts.

## How to Learn with AI Without Becoming Lazy

EduPocket audio lesson. How to learn with AI without becoming lazy.

Here is the simple rule: do not ask AI to finish the thinking before you start the thinking. Use it as a coach, not as a shortcut.

Step one. Ask for a map. Before you study a topic, ask AI to show the main ideas, the prerequisites, and the common mistakes.

Step two. Practice first. Ask AI to give you one question at a time. Answer before you read the explanation.

Step three. Ask for feedback. A good prompt is: tell me what was clear, what was weak, and what I should review next.

Step four. Keep an error log. Every mistake becomes a small lesson for tomorrow.

The goal is not to collect perfect answers. The goal is to build a repeatable loop: plan, try, get feedback, revise, and try again.

## Why Small Lessons Work Better Than Big Promises

EduPocket audio lesson. Why small lessons work better than big promises.

A small lesson is not a weak lesson. It is a focused lesson with one clear outcome.

Start with one target. What should the learner do differently after this lesson?

Show one model. Do not explain everything. Give one strong example and ask the learner to notice why it works.

Give one practice task. Keep it short enough to finish, but real enough to matter.

Then give one useful piece of feedback. Correct the highest value mistake, not every mistake.

A big promise creates excitement. A small lesson creates movement. And movement is what keeps learning alive.
