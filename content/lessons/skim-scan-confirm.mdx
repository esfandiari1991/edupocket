Skim Scan Confirm is a simple reading routine for exam tasks and academic texts.

## Step 1: Skim

Read quickly for the general topic and structure. Do not stop for unknown words.

Look for:

- title
- first and last sentence of paragraphs
- repeated words
- contrast words such as however and although

## Step 2: Scan

Search for names, numbers, dates, keywords, and synonyms from the question.

If the question says **benefit**, the text may say **advantage**, **helps**, or **useful**.

## Step 3: Confirm

Read the sentence before and after the answer. Make sure the meaning matches the question.

This step prevents the most common reading mistake: finding the same word but missing the meaning.

## Practice

Use this checklist on one paragraph today:

1. Write the main topic in five words.
2. Circle two keywords.
3. Find one synonym.
4. Confirm the answer with one full sentence.

Reading speed improves when accuracy has a process.
