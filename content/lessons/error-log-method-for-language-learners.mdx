An error log is a personal practice map. It shows what to repeat next.

## What to record

Use four columns:

| Mistake | Correction | Pattern | Next practice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| He go to work | He goes to work | third person -s | Make 5 sentences |

The pattern column is the most important one. It helps you avoid fixing only one sentence.

## When to review

Review errors on this schedule:

- same day
- next day
- three days later
- one week later

If the mistake returns, keep it active. If it disappears, archive it.

## Practice rule

Do not only read corrections. Produce new language with the same pattern.

For example:

```text
She works online.
My brother studies Arabic.
The app helps me review vocabulary.
```

The goal is not to feel bad about mistakes. The goal is to turn mistakes into a personal curriculum.
