Big promises are exciting, but small lessons are trainable.

## Episode summary

This episode explains why a focused micro-lesson can create better learning momentum than a long, unfocused study session.

## Main ideas

- One target reduces confusion.
- Short practice makes feedback faster.
- Visible progress keeps learners engaged.
- A repeated framework beats a one-time burst of motivation.

## Micro-lesson plan

Use this 12-minute pattern:

| Minute | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Target and model |
| 2-5 | Notice the pattern |
| 5-9 | Practice |
| 9-12 | Feedback and next step |

## Reflection

A small lesson should not feel shallow. It should feel precise.
