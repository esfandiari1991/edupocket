AI becomes useful when it asks you to think before it gives you the answer.

## Episode summary

This episode explains how learners can use AI for planning, practice, feedback, and reflection without outsourcing the hard parts of learning.

## Main ideas

- Ask AI to quiz you before explaining.
- Request feedback on your reasoning, not only your final answer.
- Keep an error log from each study session.
- Use AI to create practice variations.
- Verify claims when accuracy matters.

## Study prompt

After listening, try this:

```text
Ask me five questions about [topic].
Wait for my answer each time.
After each answer, tell me what I did well and what I should review.
At the end, create an error log.
```

## Reflection

AI should help you build a stronger learning process. If it only gives you finished answers, the process is too passive.
